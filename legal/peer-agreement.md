# StudSwap Peer Swap Agreement (DRAFT, NOT FOR PUBLICATION)

**⚠️ This is a working draft, not reviewed by a lawyer.**

*This agreement is formed directly between two matched StudSwap users
("Party A" and "Party B") once they finalize a swap: once dates (and
optionally a negotiated price/day) are proposed and both sides confirm.
StudSwap is not a party to this agreement and provides this template solely
as a convenience; the specific terms below (names, dates, price) are filled
in automatically from what both parties agreed to in the app.*

## 1. The Swap

- **Party A:** [Name, university], flat at [city] from [date] to [date].
- **Party B:** [Name, university], flat at [city] from [date] to [date].
  *(For a one-directional stay, only one flat and one occupying party apply;
  the other party is the payer only.)*
- **Type:** ☐ Mutual swap (both parties stay at each other's flat) ☐
  One-directional stay (one party stays, the other's flat is not used)
- **Price/day:** either party's own listed price, or a negotiated price/day
  both sides agreed to in chat before confirming.
- **Settlement:** for a mutual swap, whichever side's flat is priced lower
  owes the difference to the other; for a one-directional stay, the staying
  party owes the full stay cost to the flat owner. StudSwap calculates and
  displays this amount but never collects, holds, or transfers it — the two
  parties settle it directly between themselves, by whatever means they
  choose, using the payment details each has shared in the app.
- **Damage Deposit:** not currently collected by StudSwap for any swap.

## 2. Each Party Confirms

- They have the legal right to offer their flat for the dates listed (per
  StudSwap's Terms of Service, Section 4), and take full personal
  responsibility for this being true.
- They will leave the flat in the condition described in their listing,
  reasonably clean and with all stated amenities functional.
- They will provide access (keys, codes, or equivalent) by the agreed date
  and a point of contact in case of issues.
- They understand StudSwap is not a party to this agreement and has no
  obligation to resolve disputes between them.

## 3. During the Stay

- The occupying party agrees to treat the flat with reasonable care, comply
  with any house rules communicated by the flat owner, and not exceed the
  agreed occupancy (number of guests) without prior consent.
- Any damage or issue should be reported directly to the other party. StudSwap
  has no claims process to resolve this (see Section 5).

## 4. Changes and Cancellation

- Either party may propose different dates or a different price/day at any
  time before both sides have confirmed. This replaces the previous proposal
  and resets both confirmations.
- Confirming costs each party a flat €25 charged directly by StudSwap: €5 is
  StudSwap's non-refundable service fee, and €20 is refundable, auto-refunded
  once the stay has been underway for a day. Either party may still cancel
  after confirming, in-app, but once that auto-refund has already happened
  there's nothing left to forfeit. Before then: more than 7 days' notice
  before the stay refunds the cancelling party's own €20; 7 days' notice or
  less instead forfeits it to the other party (see StudSwap's Terms of
  Service, Section 8, for the exact mechanism, including how a forfeiture is
  actually paid out). The party who did not cancel always gets their own €20
  refunded either way, since their money was never at risk.

## 5. Dispute Resolution

Disputes should be resolved directly between the two parties. StudSwap does
not currently mediate, arbitrate, or hold any deposit related to this
agreement. This agreement does not waive either party's right to pursue
independent legal remedies.

## 6. Acknowledgment

By confirming a swap in the app, both parties acknowledge they have read and
agree to this Peer Swap Agreement and StudSwap's [Terms of Service](/terms).
