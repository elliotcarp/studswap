## About this document

This is an **entirely optional** template for an agreement **between two StudSwap users**, on top of their [Peer Swap Agreement](/peer-agreement), covering a security deposit (sometimes called a damage waiver, deductible, or "caution") the two of them may choose to exchange directly to cover accidental damage during a stay.

**StudSwap is not a party to it, does not collect, hold, verify, or enforce any deposit, and has no claims process for it.** The Peer Swap Agreement is explicit that there is no deposit by default (see its §1 and §5.4) — this template exists only for the two users who'd like one anyway, agreed and settled entirely between themselves, the same way the stay-cost settlement itself works.

Neither party is obliged to use this template or to exchange any deposit at all. Using it, or agreeing an amount different from any figure StudSwap's app suggests, is entirely up to the two of you.

**Read this before agreeing to a deposit. It only becomes binding if both of you actually agree to it, however you choose to record that (in chat, by both filling in this template, or otherwise).**

Note the parties may be resident in different countries.`

## 2. What the deposit is for

**2.1** Security against damage to the accommodation, or loss of its contents, beyond ordinary wear and tear, caused during the stay by the occupying party or anyone they let in.

**2.2** It is **not** security for the stay-cost settlement itself (see Peer Swap Agreement §2) or for either party simply changing their mind — only for damage or loss as described above.

## 3. Returning the deposit

**3.1** Absent damage, the deposit (or the undamaged portion of it) should be returned within `[X days — DECISION]` of the stay ending.

**3.2** If the host believes there's damage, they should raise it with the occupying party as soon as they notice it, with the dated photographs described below, and the two should agree a fair deduction directly. If they can't agree, either may still pursue the other through ordinary legal channels — **StudSwap has no role in resolving this.**

**3.3 Evidence matters far more than a written amount.** Both parties should take dated, time-stamped photographs (or a short video) of every room at the start and end of the stay, and share them in the in-app chat. Without this, a disagreement about what was already damaged versus what happened during the stay is very hard to resolve — for either of you, and for anyone you ask to help mediate it afterwards.

## 4. What this does not do

**4.1** It does not make StudSwap a party, a guarantor, an escrow holder, or a stakeholder of the deposit in any way. StudSwap never sees this money.

**4.2** It does not replace or override the Peer Swap Agreement, which continues to apply in full — this is an addendum to it, not a substitute.

**4.3** It does not extend, or otherwise change, the notice-based cancellation rules in the [Fees and Refunds Policy](/fees) or the Peer Swap Agreement's §6 — those govern StudSwap's own €25 charge, not this deposit.

**4.4 Insurance.** A deposit between two peers is not insurance, and doesn't necessarily cover everything a home-contents policy would. Consider whether your own insurance already covers this, on either side, before relying on a deposit instead.
