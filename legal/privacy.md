## 2. What We Collect

**Account data:** your email address (used for sign-in and verified against a
university domain allow-list), and, if you choose password sign-in, a
salted, hashed password (we never store your password in plain text).

**Profile data, all of which is visible to other users you're shown to or
matched with:** name, age, university, field of study, year of study, home
city, availability dates, how many people your flat fits, your listed price
per day, smoking/pet preferences, photos of yourself and of your flat, a
short description of yourself and of your flat, and your answers to a small
number of prompts.

**Activity data:** who you swipe on and in which direction, your matches, and
the messages you send within a match's chat.

**Balance data:** your Credit balance (see the [Terms of Service](/terms),
Section 6. Credits have no real world monetary value and cannot currently
be bought or cashed out).

**We do not currently collect:** government ID, proof of enrollment
documents, payment card details, or precise location data.

## 3. Why We Process It (Legal Basis)

- **To provide the service** (matching, messaging, showing your listing to
  others): necessary to perform our contract with you (our Terms of
  Service).
- **To verify student status:** necessary to perform that same contract
  (StudSwap is specifically for verified students).
- **To keep the platform safe** (for example, preventing duplicate or
  fraudulent accounts): our legitimate interest in running a trustworthy
  service.
