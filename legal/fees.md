## 1. What we charge

**€25 per person, charged when an arrangement is confirmed.** Nothing before that.

| | Amount | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Service fee | **€5** | Our charge for the matching and confirmation service. Our revenue. Not refunded on cancellation. |
| Refundable amount | **€20** | Refunded to you 24 hours after your stay begins. Not refunded if you cancel with 7 days' notice or less. |

We charge this once per arrangement, per person. We charge it whatever the accommodation is worth, whatever the dates, and whichever type of arrangement it is.

## 2. What we do not charge

**We take nothing from the money you pay another user.** The price difference in a swap, and the cost of a one-directional stay, are paid directly between the two of you. We never receive that money and take no percentage of it. See Section 9 of the [Terms of Service](/terms) — it matters, and it means we cannot recover it for you if something goes wrong.

We do not charge for creating an account, browsing, matching, or messaging.

## 3. The €20 — what it is and is not

The €20 is **part of our fee**, which we contractually undertake to refund to you once your stay has begun.

**It is not:**

- a deposit;
- held on your behalf, or on the other user's behalf;
- held in escrow or in trust;
- security for anything you owe the other user;
- cover for damage, theft, injury, or anything that happens during a stay;
- *arrhes*, an advance payment, or a part-payment towards anything else.

If you damage someone's flat, or someone damages yours, the €20 has nothing to do with it. That is between the two of you.

`[LEGAL] Confirm the disclaimer of arrhes/advance-payment status works in France (Article L.214-1 Code de la consommation) and check the equivalent default rule in each other country served. National presumptions about sums paid in advance differ and the French disclaimer does not automatically travel.`

## 4. When you get the €20 back

**Automatically, without asking, in all of these cases:**

- **Your stay begins** — refunded automatically 24 hours after the agreed start date. We hold it back only if the other party reports within that window that you didn't provide access or didn't turn up.
- **Either party cancels more than 7 days before the start date** — refunded to both of you.
- **The other party cancels with 7 days' notice or less** — refunded to you, plus €20 compensation (Section 5).
- **Something goes wrong on our side** — you get the full €25 back (Section 6).

**You do not get it back if you cancel with 7 days' notice or less.** That is the deterrent working as intended.

Refunds go to your original payment method. If it is no longer available, we will agree an alternative with you.

## 5. Compensation when someone cancels on you

If the other party cancels with 7 days' notice or less, **we pay you €20** on top of refunding your own.

- This is **our** obligation to you, funded from the fee we charged the person who cancelled. It is not their money passed through us.
- We pay it by bank transfer to the details you gave us, within `[X]` days. You do not need to ask.
- It has nothing to do with any money you may have already paid that person directly. **We cannot recover that** — see Section 7.

## 6. If we get it wrong

If an arrangement cannot go ahead because of a fault on our side — a defect in the Service, a wrongful suspension, or a payment failure attributable to us — **we refund the full €25**, including the service fee, to both parties.

This is a right, not a gesture. It does not limit any other remedy you have.

## 7. What we cannot refund

**We cannot refund, recover, or compensate you for money you paid directly to another user.** We never had it. If a stay is cancelled after you have already paid them, ask them for it back. If they refuse, your recourse is against them — by agreement or through the courts.

The €20 is not related to that amount and does not cover it.
